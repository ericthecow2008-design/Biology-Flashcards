     INTRODUCTION TO CELL BIOLOGY
                         &
                   THE CELL THEORY
                                                                                                   1
This module introduces cell biology and the cell theory. We will look at a brief history of cell biology,
how cells are viewed under the microscope, and examine the cell theory, a pillar of modern biology.
The basic components of prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells will be covered. A discussion on the tree of
life and the cellular features of different groups of organisms will be followed by a discussion on model
organisms.
Learning Outcomes:
• Explain why the cell is the fundamental unit of life and explain the three tenets of the cell theory.
• Explain the basic properties of cells and the close relationship between structure and function in biological
   systems.
• Compare and contrast various features of prokaryotic cells and eukaryotic cells.
• Describe how model organisms are used to study and understand biological processes.
