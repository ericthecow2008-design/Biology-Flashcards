An overview of metabolism will be explored in this module. The basic forms of energy (kinetic and
potential energy) will be discussed followed by a review of the first and second laws of thermodynamics

as they govern energy flow in biological systems. The final section of the module will examine chemical
reactions,

emphasizing the function of enzymes.

Learning Outcomes:
• Describe energy flow in anabolic and catabolic metabolism

• Contrast kinetic and potential forms of energy and relate the structure of ATP to its role in cellular energy
interactions

• Explain the laws of thermodynamics as they apply to biological systems

• Describe the type(s) of energy involved in chemical reactions and the role of enzymes.
