The topic of nucleic acids and information will be covered in this module. A brief review of nucleotides
and phosphodiester bonds will be followed by the 3-D structure of DNA. The Central Dogma will be
discussed, which will be followed by the process of transcription in both prokaryotes and eukaryotes.

• Describe the chemical composition and structure of DNA and analyze how it stores and transmits
information.
•

• Explain the significance of RNA processing of a primary transcript to messenger RNA.
