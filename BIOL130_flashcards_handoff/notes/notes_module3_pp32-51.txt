This module begins by exploring what cell membranes are made of and how phospholipids come
together for form the phospholipid bilayer that surrounds cells. The fluid mosaic model will be
discussed followed an examination of the endomembrane system and other membrane bound
organelles, including the endosymbiont origin of mitochondria and chloroplasts. The module will end

• Describe the composition of cell membranes.
• Explain the relationship between the structure of the eukaryotic endomembrane system and the function of
each of the membrane-bound compartments.
•

• Describe the mechanism behind the selective permeability of membranes and the reasons why certain
substances can pass through them while others cannot.
